# Instant Technical Diagnostics for Publisher Ad Stacks.

When ad creatives fail to render, line items under-deliver, or CMP consent signals break, publishers risk immediate revenue loss and user drop-off. Aeli AdOps provides certified ad tech engineering to diagnose, troubleshoot, and fix ad stack issues rapidly.

![Technical Support AdTech Issue Diagnostics](/images/technical-support-hero.jpg)

## Core Technical Support Areas

### Ad Rendering & GPT Tag Debugging
Trace ad calls live in Google Publisher Console, resolve GPT tag syntax errors, and fix iframe sandbox restrictions causing blank ad containers.

### Line Item Pacing & Delivery Audits
Diagnose under-delivering guaranteed direct campaigns, resolve key-value syntax conflicts, and fix targeting audience mismatches.

### Prebid & Latency Optimization
Audit Prebid wrapper timeouts, clear main-thread blocking scripts, and optimize server-to-server (S2S) header bidding routing.

### Ad Quality & Safety Governance
Audit third-party VAST/VPAID tags, block heavy auto-redirect creatives, and enforce SSL and heavy-ad capping rules in Google Ad Manager.
