# Every Impression Has Value. Let's Unlock Its True Potential.

Yield optimization is the strategic discipline of structuring ad pricing, demand competition, and layout health to extract maximum total revenue per available impression—without compromising reader experience. Aeli AdOps helps publishers analyze ad telemetry and implement data-driven yield strategies.

![Every Impression Has Value - Yield Optimization](/images/every-impression-has-value-yield.jpg)

## Core Focus Areas

### Dynamic Pricing Strategy
Establish granular Unified Pricing Rules (UPR) in Google Ad Manager by country tier, device type, and session depth to capture true buyer willingness-to-pay.

### Multi-Demand Auction Competition
Force direct campaigns, Google AdX, open auctions, header bidding partners, and PMPs to compete on equal footing for every ad request in real time.

### Viewability & Layout Engineering
Position ad containers in natural reading focus zones, deploy sticky footers, and configure lazy loading offsets to elevate viewability past 80%.

### Fill Rate & Revenue Balancing
Prevent penny-bids from diluting CPM performance while maintaining optimal inventory fill rates across all traffic channels.

## Optimization Is a Continuous Process

Advertising markets change. Audience behavior shifts. Demand partners update algorithm parameters.

That's why we approach yield optimization as a continuous cycle of analysis, dynamic floor testing, performance auditing, and revenue scaling.
