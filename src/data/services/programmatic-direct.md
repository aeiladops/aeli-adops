# Bridge Your Inventory Directly to High-Value Buyers.

Programmatic Direct combines direct sales pricing control with automated programmatic execution. By packaging your premium inventory into specialized programmatic deals, you command higher CPMs from agency buyers while eliminating manual billing and tags.

![Programmatic Direct Inventory and Demand Connections](/images/programmatic-direct-hero.jpg)

## Programmatic Direct Channels

### Programmatic Guaranteed (PG)
Fixed CPM pricing with 100% volume commitments. Eliminates manual tagging while securing direct brand revenues.

### Preferred Deals (PD)
Unreserved fixed-price deals giving premium buyers a first look at valuable audience impressions before open auction bidding.

### Private Marketplaces (PMP)
Invite-only auctions packaging high-viewability inventory and first-party audience segments into targeted Deal IDs with premium floors.
