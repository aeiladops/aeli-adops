# Your Dedicated Engine Room for Publisher Monetization.

Ad Operations (AdOps) is the operational heart of digital publishing. From inputting complex campaign line items and auditing creative tags to monitoring impression pacing and resolving SSP reporting gaps, AdOps ensures every impression yields maximum commercial value.

![Managed Ad Operations Wheel Engine](/images/ad-operations-hero.jpg)

## The 8-Stage AdOps Engine

### 1. Campaign Setup & Line Item Trafficking
Inputting orders, flight dates, targeting parameters, impression caps, and priority tiers inside Google Ad Manager.

### 2. Precision Targeting & Key-Values
Configuring custom Key-Values, geo-fencing, device targeting, and first-party audience segments.

### 3. Creative Management & QA
Auditing HTML5 banners, third-party JavaScript tags, and VAST video tags for SSL compliance, layout integrity, and heavy script limits.

### 4. Real-Time Delivery Pacing
Monitoring guaranteed direct campaign impression delivery daily to prevent under-delivery or unexpected pacing spikes.

### 5. Quality & Viewability Governance
Blocking malicious auto-redirects, enforcing viewability targets (70%+), and preventing layout shifts.

### 6. Automated Reporting & Discrepancies
Aggregating daily impression logs and resolving 10%+ reporting gaps between GAM and third-party SSP dashboards.
