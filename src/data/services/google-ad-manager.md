# Master Your Ad Server. Maximize Every Impression.

Google Ad Manager (GAM) is the central nerve system of modern publisher ad operations. Combining ad serving capabilities with programmatic exchange access (Google AdX), GAM provides the granular control needed to manage direct campaigns, programmatic demand partners, and private market deals.

![Google Ad Manager 360 Ecosystem](/images/google-ad-manager-hero.jpg)

## Strategic GAM Modules

### Clean Inventory Hierarchy
Structure logical Ad Units, Placements, and custom Key-Values to enable precise advertiser targeting without inflating account complexity.

### Unified Pricing Rules (UPR)
Establish dynamic CPM floor prices by geographic region, device environment, and content vertical to capture maximum buyer willingness-to-pay.

### Line Item & Priority Optimization
Balance Priority 4 (Sponsorship), Priority 8 (Standard), Priority 12 (Price Priority), and AdX Dynamic Allocation for real-time auction competition.

### Creative Governance & Safety
Enforce strict heavy-ad capping rules, SSL validation, and Google Ad Review Center filtering to protect site latency and brand safety.
