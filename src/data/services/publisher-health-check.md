# Comprehensive Diagnostic for Your Publishing Business.

A Publisher Health Check provides a holistic audit of your ad tech stack, inventory structure, pricing floors, demand setup, and viewability metrics. We identify silent revenue leaks and technical bottlenecks before they impact your yield.

![Publisher Health Check](/images/ns-img-1.png)

## What We Assess

### Monetization Architecture
In-depth evaluation of your Google Ad Manager configuration, header bidding setup, and ad tag integrations.

### Yield & Revenue Leaks
Pinpoint underperforming ad units, sub-optimal price floors, and unmonetized inventory.

### Viewability & User Experience
Measure viewability metrics against industry benchmarks and balance ad density with site speed.

### Demand Partner Performance
Analyze SSP fill rates, latency, timeout settings, and bidder competition efficiency.
